# Business model — CAF

Independent public-sector market-entry compliance for Central African Republic.

- Ministry of Finance and Budget (Direction Générale de la Commande
  Publique / Autorité de Régulation de la Commande Publique) public
  procurement -- Code de la Commande Publique (Loi N° 25.016 du 23
  décembre 2025), which replaces the 2008 ARMP/DGMP structure; no
  self-service e-procurement portal, notices/texts published directly
  on finances.gouv.cf/marches-publics/* (see `src/marketentry/facts.cljc`)
- RCCM (Registre du Commerce et du Crédit Mobilier) business
  registration under OHADA's Acte Uniforme relatif au Droit Commercial
  Général, per the Ministry of Justice's own service listing
  (justice.gouv.cf)
- Direction Générale des Impôts et des Domaines (DGID) tax
  registration, Ministry of Finance and Budget
- Code de la Commande Publique "Marché réservé" (reserved-contract)
  gate -- bars a non-eligible bidder from a contract reserved for
  social-economy actors or SMEs meeting a workforce-composition
  threshold (>=30% persons with disabilities, or >=50% youth, or >=50%
  women)
