# Operator guide — CAF

Portal: no self-service e-procurement portal -- notices/texts are
published directly on the Ministry of Finance and Budget's own website
(finances.gouv.cf/marches-publics/*), regulated by A.R.C.O.P (Autorité
de Régulation de la Commande Publique) and D.G.C.O.P (Direction
Générale de la Commande Publique) under the Code de la Commande
Publique (Loi N° 25.016 du 23 décembre 2025). Entity: RCCM (OHADA
AUDCG) + DGID tax record. Reserved-market (Marché réservé) engagements
also clear the Code's own workforce-composition/legal-form eligibility
gate before a filing can proceed. Human-gated filing only.
